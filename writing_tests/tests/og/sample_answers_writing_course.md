# SECTION A — Error Correction & Micro-Writing
## Intermediate — Error Correction
1. Thank you for your email. I am checking the matter but need more information from my colleague.
2. The attached file is not the same version that we discussed in the last meeting.
3. Please confirm whether the applicant has already submitted the missing documents.
4. We will update you on the situation as soon as possible, but today is difficult because we have many tasks.
5. I would appreciate it if you could check the points below again and give me your advice.

## Advanced — Error Correction
1. Thank you for your email. I am reviewing the matter but require additional information from my colleague.
2. The attached file is not the same version that we discussed during the last meeting.
3. Please confirm whether the applicant has already submitted the missing documents.
4. We will update you as soon as possible, but today is challenging due to a heavy workload.
5. I would appreciate it if you could review the points below and provide your advice.

## Intermediate — Micro-Writing
6. I would like to inform you that we need two additional days to prepare our response.
7. Could you please clarify the part of the guideline that appears to contradict the earlier version?
8. Please find attached a corrected version of the notice.

## Advanced — Micro-Writing
6. Please note that we will require two additional days to prepare our response.
7. Could you clarify the section of the guideline that appears to conflict with the earlier instructions?
8. Please find attached the corrected notice for your review.

# SECTION B — Task-Based Writing
## Pre-Test (Version A) — Intermediate Email
Subject: Inquiry Regarding Document Submission Requirements

Dear Ms. Müller,

I hope you are well. I am writing to ask for clarification about a difference in the document submission requirements that we recently noticed. According to our information, one version states that applicants must submit the form at the time of filing, while another source says it may be submitted later.

Could you please confirm which procedure is correct? We would appreciate your response by Thursday, as we are preparing guidance materials.

Thank you for your assistance.

Kind regards,
[Name]

## Pre-Test — Intermediate Administrative Explanation
From this month, an additional step has been added to the process for handling foreign applicants. Staff must now check whether the applicant has previously submitted related documents before beginning the review. This change reduces delays caused by repeated requests.

When processing a new case, please check the internal database first and confirm the submission history.

## Pre-Test — Advanced Administrative Explanation
A new preliminary check has been added to the workflow for foreign applicants. Staff must confirm whether the applicant has previously submitted related documents before beginning the standard review. This aims to reduce redundant requests and ensure consistency.

Please confirm submission history in the internal system and promptly request any missing documents.

# SECTION C — Revision & Editing
## Pre-Test — Intermediate Revision
Dear Colleague,

I reviewed your draft, but several parts were unclear, and I could not understand the procedure. The applicant has contacted us twice, and we have not been able to respond because we do not yet have an accurate explanation.

Could you please review the instructions and provide the correct procedure? As the deadline is approaching, we would appreciate receiving the updated version soon.

Thank you.

## Pre-Test — Advanced Revision
Dear Colleague,

I reviewed your draft and found several unclear sections, which made it difficult to determine the correct procedure. The applicant has contacted us twice, but we cannot respond appropriately without accurate information.

Please review the instructions again and share a revised version outlining the correct steps. As the deadline is near, we would appreciate receiving the update as soon as possible.

Best regards.

# SECTION A — Error Correction & Micro-Writing
## Intermediate — Error Correction
1. We received your question but cannot reply until the responsible person gives us more detail.
2. The information in your message is different from what we received from WIPO last week.
3. Please advise me whether the applicant has already paid the required fee.
4. The meeting has been postponed because some participants were not available.
5. I kindly request that you check the attached document again and let me know your opinion.

## Advanced — Error Correction
1. We received your inquiry but cannot respond until the responsible officer provides additional details.
2. The information you provided differs from the update we received from WIPO last week.
3. Please let me know whether the applicant has already paid the required fee.
4. The meeting has been postponed because several participants were unavailable.
5. I would appreciate it if you could review the attached document and share your comments.

## Intermediate — Micro-Writing
6. I would like to inform you that we need to move the scheduled meeting to next week.
7. Could you please clarify the part of the guideline where the two versions seem to conflict?
8. Please find attached a summary of the key points from our recent internal meeting.

## Advanced — Micro-Writing
6. Please be informed that the scheduled meeting must be postponed until next week.
7. Could you clarify the section of the guideline in which the two versions appear to contradict each other?
8. Please find attached a summary of the key points discussed during our recent internal meeting.
