# Phase 5 Unit 1 Activity Rewrite Review

Date: 2026-06-09

Source:
- `int/md/working/aw-int-all_0519.md`
- Phase 5 standard in `int/edits & guides/full-review/aw-int_print_readiness_repair_plan_0530.md`

Scope:
- Unit 1 only
- activity-level rewrite review
- focus on whether each activity follows the Phase 5 rewrite standard:
  - short clear title
  - 1-2 sentences of purpose or situation
  - clear task instruction
  - item list only when needed
  - concise reminder only when it supports the task
  - placeholder without redundant `Write Here` language

## Overall Judgment

Unit 1 is broadly usable and already stronger than many later units, but it does not follow a fully consistent Phase 5 rewrite standard yet.

Main pattern:
- most production activities are clear and workable
- several support/example sections are split into multiple short divs, which weakens activity cohesion
- some titles are still generic rather than purpose-led
- some support text is slightly longer or more layered than the Phase 5 target

## Activity Review

### A. Warm-Up

Status: `Needs light tightening`

What works:
- `Sentence Comparison` is a clear title
- task is immediate and easy to understand
- example table matches the warm-up purpose

What to improve:
- `Quick Check` is useful, but it reads more like a support note than part of the same warm-up flow
- consider whether the warm-up should be one integrated activity block or two more clearly separated steps

### B. Example Check

Status: `Needs rewrite consolidation`

What works:
- the weak/strong contrast is clear
- `Why This Works` explains the reader effect directly

What to improve:
- this section is fragmented across four short divs: `Look at the Stronger Sentence`, `Compare`, `example-bad/example-good`, `Why This Works`
- the two `notice` divs are especially thin and could likely be merged into one clearer lead-in
- Phase 5 favors one compact activity shape over stacked micro-divs

### C. Language

Status: `Mostly pass`

What works:
- `Basic Sentence Pattern`, `Practice: Build Clear Sentences`, and `Simplify the Sentence` are clear and teachable
- placeholders are justified and row counts are appropriate
- no redundant `Write Here` labels

What to improve:
- `Practice: Identify the Parts` gives a task but no response space; that may be acceptable for oral/mark-up work, but it should be intentionally treated as a no-placeholder activity
- `Simplification Strategies` is useful, but slightly dense for the compact Phase 5 target

### D. Guided Practice

Status: `Pass`

What works:
- strong title
- situation appears before the task
- output length is clear
- support list is directly tied to the writing task

No immediate rewrite issue noted.

### E. Freer Practice

Status: `Pass`

What works:
- concise scenario
- clear output expectation
- support bullets help without giving the answer

No immediate rewrite issue noted.

### F. Editing Practice

Status: `Pass with minor wording heaviness`

What works:
- clear editing purpose
- problem categories are easy to use
- task instruction is explicit

What to improve:
- the activity introduction is now much better than before, but the activity still has a fair amount of prefatory framing before the student reaches the actual work

### G. Self-Check and Reflection

Status: `Pass`

What works:
- self-check comes after production/editing
- reflection questions are clear
- placeholder is justified

No immediate rewrite issue noted.

### H. Homework & Extension

Status: `Pass with structural note`

What works:
- the first `write` div is a clean scaffold from short sentences to mini-message
- the second `write` div clearly extends the skill into a real-work or adapted task

What to improve:
- there are two separate `write` divs under the same visible section heading, which is acceptable, but should be watched for consistency across later units

## Priority Follow-Up for Unit 1

1. Merge or simplify the fragmented `B. Example Check` stack into a more compact single-flow activity.
2. Decide whether `A. Warm-Up` should remain as two linked sub-steps or be tightened into one clearer activity flow.
3. Confirm whether no-placeholder tasks in `C. Language` are intentionally oral/markup tasks or need explicit response handling.

## Phase 5 Outcome

Unit 1 is not a major rewrite case, but it still needs a small Phase 5 cleanup pass before it can be treated as fully aligned to the activity-level rewrite standard.
