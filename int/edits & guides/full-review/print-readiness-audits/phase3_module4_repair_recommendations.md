## Executive Summary

Module 4 remains in the older unrepaired structure. It repeats the same obsolete scaffold found in Module 3 before repair:

1. Duplicate concept-primer `A` headings before `A. Warm-Up`.
2. `F. Self-Check Before Freer Practice` placed before `E. Freer Practice`.
3. Extra `E. Extended Writing Task` sections after `H. Transfer Extension`.
4. Separate `H. Transfer Extension` and `H. Homework` headings.
5. Generic response labels such as `Write Here`, `Use this box`, `Write Your Revised Version`, `Write Your Paragraph`, and `Homework Draft`.
6. Many placeholders outside activity divs.
7. Mini examples wrapped as separate `example` divs in ways that create nested-div risk and visual clutter.

The main pedagogical issue is not only structure. Units 12-15 are about audience control for outward-facing writing, but the activities frequently repeat the same support ideas in lists, mini examples, self-checks, and extended writing tasks. The repair should preserve each unit's useful genre focus while simplifying the teaching path:

1. Compare weak and strong external-facing examples.
2. Learn the key pattern or language.
3. Do controlled sentence/email practice.
4. Write one guided text from a clear situation.
5. Write one or two freer texts with separate response spaces.
6. Edit a weak text.
7. Self-check and reflect.
8. Extend or complete homework under one `H. Homework & Extension` section.

## Unit 12: Writing Simple External Inquiries

### Required Structural Repair

- Merge `### A. What Is an External Inquiry?` into `### A. Warm-Up`.
- Keep one visible `A. Warm-Up`, one `B. Example Check`, and one `C. Language`.
- Replace `F. Self-Check Before Freer Practice` with `G. Self-Check and Reflection` after the editing task.
- Move `Transfer Extension`, `Extended Writing Task`, and `Homework` into one `### H. Homework & Extension`.
- Move all response placeholders inside their relevant divs.

### Pedagogical / Language Repair

- The word `clarification` is central to the unit but is advanced for intermediate learners. Keep it if pedagogically necessary, but introduce it with a short support phrase such as `clarification means making an unclear point clear`.
- The concept-primer example is useful, but the email should show clearer email formatting with subject, greeting, body, closing, and sign-off as separate lines if it is meant as an email model.
- `A Good External Inquiry`, `Example Check`, and `Why This Works` repeat the same ideas. Merge into one concise explanation.
- `Language 1. Structure of an External Inquiry` is useful but overloaded. Keep the structure list and phrase bank, but remove or convert the mini inquiry example unless it genuinely supports a different task.
- Remove the `Teaching Point` div. Its content can become one short sentence in the language section.
- `Practice A` and `Practice B` both have item-level placeholders followed by redundant aggregate placeholders. Remove the aggregate placeholders.
- The `Mini example` blocks are over-wrapped in separate `example` divs and create nested-div risk. Convert them to plain prose or one explicit neutral example div where needed.
- Freer Practice asks students to write two inquiry emails but provides only one `U12-freer-document` placeholder. Either split into two placeholders or reduce the task to one inquiry. Based on Modules 2-3 repair decisions, split placeholders are better if the task keeps two emails.
- Editing Practice expects a full corrected inquiry but gives only `rows=2`; increase the response space if students write a complete short email.
- The internal-note transfer task and inquiry paragraph are related. Keep only the stronger extension path or combine them under `H. Homework & Extension`.

### Answer-Key Implications

- Update headings to the repaired `A-H` sequence.
- If freer practice keeps two emails, add open-ended criteria for both.
- Move the extension/internal-note or inquiry-paragraph guidance under `H. Homework & Extension`.

## Unit 13: Providing Simple Explanations

### Required Structural Repair

- Merge `### A. What Is a Simple Explanation?` into `### A. Warm-Up`.
- Normalize to one visible `A-H` sequence.
- Move `Transfer Extension`, `Extended Writing Task`, and `Homework` into `### H. Homework & Extension`.
- Move all placeholders inside divs.

### Pedagogical / Language Repair

- The Focus contains both short explanatory text and report-style paragraph aims. Decide whether the unit is teaching short explanation emails/notes, report-style paragraphs, or both. The current sequence blurs these.
- The warm-up asks students to identify missing information but gives inline numbered blanks rather than a proper response pattern. Use end blanks or a compact placeholder inside the div.
- `A Useful Explanation Helps the Reader`, `Example Check`, and `Why This Works` repeat background/reason/effect. Merge the repeated teaching point.
- The language section phrase bank is useful, but `Background -> Reason -> Effect` should not use raw arrow notation; use prose or a proper list.
- Remove redundant aggregate placeholders after item-level placeholders.
- `Practice B` has a numbered list immediately after another numbered checklist without spacing or clear transition. Reorder as source sentence -> task -> placeholder.
- Guided Practice gives a situation, but the task may require students to invent too much. Add a small fact bank or keep the existing facts but make the expected explanation path explicit.
- Freer Practice asks for two explanations and already has two placeholders; keep that pattern but put labels and placeholders inside the `write` div.
- Editing Practice row count is too small for a corrected explanation.
- The two-reader transfer and report-style paragraph overlap. Keep both only if they serve distinct learner actions: audience adjustment versus extended report paragraph. Otherwise merge under H.

### Answer-Key Implications

- Unit 13 answer key is currently thin and has only two sample answers for Practice A, even though the source has more items. Add full coverage after source repair.
- Add guidance for Practice B, Guided Practice, Freer Practice, Editing Practice, Self-Check/Reflection, and H.

## Unit 15: Maintaining Consistent Email Style

### Required Structural Repair

- Editing Practice gives item-level placeholders and then asks for a full corrected version. Decide whether the task is item-level rewrite or full corrected version. Do not keep both unless the compiled version is a distinct final output.
