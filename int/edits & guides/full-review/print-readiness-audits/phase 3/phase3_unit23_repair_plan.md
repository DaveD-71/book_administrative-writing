# Phase 3 Unit 23 Repair Plan

Date: 2026-06-09

Scope:
- `int/md/working/aw-int-all_0519.md`
- `int/md/working/aw-int-answer-key.md`

## Diagnosis

Unit 23 is still in the unrepaired capstone pattern:

- visible headings are misordered and duplicated: `A B C F E D G H` with subordinate `#### A/E/F/G/H`
- the portfolio-definition primer incorrectly carries the visible `A` slot instead of the book-wide `A. Warm-Up`
- the three revision tasks are split awkwardly across `C` while the new-document task and final submission logic are scattered later
- self-check, portfolio balance, rubric, reflection, transfer, and homework functions are fragmented across old subordinate `E/F/G/H` blocks
- generic labels remain (`Write Here`, `Write Your Revised Version`, `Homework Draft`)
- the editing placeholder does not match the task, because the task asks for a revision explanation but the box is too small and framed as a full rewrite
- the answer key only contains a generic open-ended note and does not mirror the repaired activity sequence

## Repair Direction

Rebuild Unit 23 into a stable capstone `A-H` shell while preserving its portfolio-specific function:

1. `A. Warm-Up`
   - combine the portfolio definition with draft selection
   - keep `A` as the visible opening activity
2. `B. Example Check`
   - turn the portfolio-purpose explanation into a short learner-facing priority check
3. `C. Language`
   - keep the editing checklist and revision order as the explicit support layer
4. `D. Guided Practice`
   - group the three supported revision tasks for earlier writing under one visible revision section
5. `E. Freer Practice`
   - combine the assembly board, the new-document task, and the final four-piece portfolio submission under the main capstone production layer
6. `F. Editing Practice`
   - use the rebuild routine for the weakest piece and require a short revision-decision explanation rather than another duplicate full rewrite
7. `G. Self-Check and Reflection`
   - move final submission checks, balance/range checks, rubric use, and reflection into the proper post-production review layer
8. `H. Homework & Extension`
   - keep the next-time writing plan plus one final third-revision homework task

## Answer-Key Requirements

- replace the generic Unit 23 note with heading-by-heading guidance for `A-H`
- keep all teacher guidance open-ended, because students use their own earlier writing as source material
- add explicit success criteria for:
  - source-piece selection
  - revision quality across the three guided pieces
  - the new Unit 23 document
  - the final four-piece portfolio set
  - the revision-decision explanation
  - the reflection / extension / homework tasks
