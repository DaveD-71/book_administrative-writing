## Findings

### 1. Visible shell was broken

Unit 16 still had duplicate visible `A`, `E`, `F`, and `H` sections, with `F` before `E`.

### 2. The concept primer was mislabeled as visible `A`

`A. What Does "Accuracy" Mean in Writing?` functioned as support explanation, not as the book-wide opening activity.

### 3. Response spaces sat outside activity divs

Warm-up, Practice B, guided practice, freer practice, editing practice, extended writing, and transfer placeholders were outside the relevant activity divs.

### 4. Generic response labels remained

The unit still used labels such as `Write Your Corrections`, `Corrected version`, `Write Here`, `Write Your Revised Version`, `Write Your Paragraph`, and `Planning Notes`.

### 5. `E. Freer Practice` was pedagogically incomplete

Students were asked to produce corrected versions without being given actual flawed source texts to edit.

### 6. `G. Editing Practice` was under-specified and under-sized

The task asked students to explain two corrections, but it provided one `rows=2` box for the whole activity.

### 7. `F/G/H` functional layers were misassigned

The pre-freer checklist, paragraph editing, reflection, transfer, and homework functions were distributed under the wrong visible headings.

### 8. The answer key was badly out of sync

The original Unit 16 answer-key block gave only a short instructor note for `A` and one compressed note for `G`.

## Repair Plan

7. Move self-check and reflection into visible `G`.
8. Combine transfer routine and homework under visible `H. Homework & Extension`.
9. Move all placeholders inside the correct activity divs.

2. Provide model corrections for the controlled tasks.
3. Provide guided-practice and freer-practice guidance aligned to the repaired source texts.
