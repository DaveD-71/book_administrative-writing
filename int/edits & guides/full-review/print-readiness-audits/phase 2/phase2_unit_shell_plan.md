## Global Shell Rules

1. Keep `### Focus` outside the A-H activity shell.
2. Each ordinary unit should end with one visible sequence that is teachable by reference: `A B C D E F G H`.
3. If a concept primer was originally the first `A` section, keep it as the structural `### A.` and demote later `A. Warm-Up` material inside that section as a subordinate activity.
4. Move freer production before editing practice where the current source shows `D F E G H`; the final sequence should read as drafting, editing, self-check/reflection, transfer.
5. Combine repeated self-check/reflection material into one visible `G` area unless the unit needs a documented exception.
6. Combine `H. Transfer Extension` and `H. Homework` under one visible `H` area, with homework as a subordinate label when it adds value.
7. Convert duplicate `D` and `E` headings into subordinate div titles or body labels inside the relevant activity rather than leaving repeated visible letters.
8. For Module 6, preserve document-pack labels but make the visible top-level sequence teachable.

## Module Review Workshop Shell

Module review workshops are not ordinary units. Their Phase 2 shell target is:

1. title-case all visible review headings
2. keep one clear heading per review activity
3. remove stacked title rows where the `###` heading repeats the div title
4. preserve cumulative transfer tasks and strategy maps
5. calibrate response boxes after the activity instructions are stable
